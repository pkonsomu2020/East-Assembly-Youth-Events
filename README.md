# KAG East Assembly Youth Ministry Website

The official website for the KAG East Assembly Youth Ministry: a home for everything happening in the youth community, from weekly events to Camp Ignite 2026.

## What This Site Does

- **Home**: an overview of the ministry, upcoming highlights, and a live countdown to the next event.
- **Youth Events**: browse upcoming events (Youth Dinner, Youth Retreat, Worship Experience, Chill Out), register, and pay via M-Pesa. Registering requires a free account, and registrants get an automatic reminder email on the day of the event.
- **Merchandise Store**: order hoodies, T-shirts, jerseys, caps, and more, with payment via M-Pesa.
- **Camp Ignite 2026**: create an account to track camp payments over time, with a visual progress meter that fills up as installments are confirmed.
- **Volunteer Sign-Up**: join a ministry team (Media, Worship, Ushers, Hospitality, Evangelism, Photography, and more).

A companion **Admin Hub**, a separate internal dashboard, is used by ministry leadership to review registrations, orders, and payments, and to verify M-Pesa payments as they come in.

## How Payments Work

Every payment (event tickets, merchandise, camp installments) is confirmed manually: a member pastes their M-Pesa confirmation message when they pay, and a member of the team checks it against the actual till statement before marking it verified. Nothing is auto-approved, so there's always a human check on real money.

## Design

A warm, energetic look built around the ministry's flame and sky-blue branding, meant to feel current and youthful while staying rooted in the ministry's identity.
